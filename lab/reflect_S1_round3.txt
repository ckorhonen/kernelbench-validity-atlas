1. What the five findings collectively say the paper was missing — and what it did NOT miss.
Collectively the five findings are a single diagnosis: the paper had a strong scientific core and a weak
delivery layer. The numbers were reproduced exactly by the reviewers (means, t-statistics, counts, the
population residual) — the arithmetic was never in dispute. What was in dispute was the interpretive
layer: (a) claims stated where only a diagnostic was measured, (b) a packaging layer that described
experiments without containing them, (c) a total-time ranking asserted from the wrong averaging
population, and (d) a projection result presented as a boundary law. So the paper was missing:
- Separation of criteria (F3): benchmark agreement is a binary candidate-versus-reference check; frac_over
  and rho_max are a diagnostic on the reference; a relaxed application contract is a declared candidate
  decision. All three must be reported separately, never merged.
- A runnable package (F1/F4): shipped scripts that execute from inside the package, with the exact
  parameters they used, seed-derived inputs, gzip-aware row-streams, and determinism checks that re-run
  the delivered hand computation. The reviewer could reproduce the arithmetic without being able to
  reproduce a single experiment.
- Correctness-conditioned cost accounting (F5): cost quoted separately over passing rows and over all
  attempted rows, failing rows named with budget ratios, fallback policy stated, no total-time ordering
  claim.
- Honest scope (F2): the 0.604-vs-0.5625 majority values are a nearest-centroid on four log coordinates;
  they do not establish that engine class is unpredictable in principle, and they do not refute a simple
  predictive surface. The manuscript now says that.

2. What the paper did NOT miss, and what I will not let the next round of editing claim.
- Correctness of the core numbers. The reviewers reproduced the paired differences, the t-statistics,
  the 0.552% population residual, the break-even denominators (red b1 6/195.0 b2 7/225.4 b4 15/141.4 b8
  21/232.3; conv exactly zero). No re-measurement of these is warranted.
- The two-class certificate. The winograd/TF32 split survived its own discriminator (a7b, A8b, S56); the
  retraction that knocked it down (A6f's "invisible to every precision flag") was mis-scoped to
  stride-1-only rows. The corrected claim — engine class is a dispatch decision over (C, output extent)
  — is the strongest scientific finding in the package.
- The population confirmatory collapse (A3, P90_fresh 15.3584 <= frozen 16.1783, 420/420): the
  confirmatory commitment for the population result is met and shipped (pop_confirm.json).

3. What I will do differently next time, stated as rules.
Rule 1 — every shipped script executes from inside the package at build time; the build asserts exit 0.
Rule 2 — every results row carries (candidate, declared reference realization, anchor, budget ratio) and
no claim about the candidate's correctness is written without the benchmark's own allclose verdict.
Rule 3 — a claim about a ratio is written as "ratio between two arms that both miss the contract" with
the pair (rho_max, frac_over) always reported, never a single speedup number without its diagnostic pair.
Rule 4 — the interpretation of a measurement states the mechanism only where a control disproved the
alternative: the geometry term survived its control (fixed-n spread), the cudnn-path attribution survived
its discriminator (cudnn disabled), the two-class surface survived the width grid, and the
engine-class-flips-research the anchor-invariance question is the only remaining attribution in the
program that has not yet been tested against a discriminator. That is the open question, and it is the
one this reflection is designed to pursue.
