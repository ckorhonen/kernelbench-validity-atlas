**The restriction.** My representation treats admission as a binary verdict per arm, at one recorded reference realization, with seeds that are a pure function of shape. Nothing in KernelBench requires that. The task asks whether an optimization holds under a tolerance; the shipped gate is a point check, but the scientific object — a validity domain — is a probability of failing the contract over the input distribution. The binary excludes three things I have been reading as structure: partial failure rates (P3's 56/56 mixed shapes are compatible with input dependence, not just candidate dependence), expected regret as a decision quantity, and any continuous account of the fresh-process/warm confound, which can only flip a label, never move it.
