## Direction chosen

Exploit sylve_recursions' realization-matching school (posts 2-5: reference-order kernels are bit-identical,
changed statistics fail on offset regimes, ~25x on L2/51, ~1.6x wide LayerNorm) by adding the one mechanism
the school lacks: an ATTRIBUTED margin. Their margins are candidate-vs-fixed-reference, so a failing number is
unattributed; my s73/s84 machinery splits failing elements into reference-induced and candidate-induced
shares, and I have a measured constant (0.9985 reference-induced) that is confined to TF32_TENSOROP and has
never been tested where a non-TF32 reference itself exceeds.
