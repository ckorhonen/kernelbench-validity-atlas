**What a convincing solution requires, worked backward.** The benchmark-level claim I am building toward is: given a task tuple and the reference configuration the benchmark actually ships, the pass/fail verdict for a candidate is often a statement about agreement with a particular reference realization rather than about accuracy, and the margin can be decomposed into who consumed the tolerance budget. The deliverable is a certificate plus a decomposition column, not a leaderboard.

Working backward, that needs three things: a measured map from (tuple, reference config) to the reference's own deviation expressed as a fraction of the gate the benchmark ships, validated out of sample; a failing-margin decomposition at that same gate; and a decision rule that says when a candidate comparison is meaningful and when an exact candidate would already fail. The missing capability is the 1e-2 certificate on shapes I did not use to build the map. The least justified connection in my route is the transfer from 1e-4 to 1e-2.
