## Target

A benchmark-wide, quantitative validity map of the evaluator itself: for each KernelBench task, the reference's own
deviation as a fraction of the admissible budget at the shipped 1e-2 gate and the strict 1e-4 gate, with a derived
coordinate that predicts it from declared features (engine class, accumulation depth K, |A|max/atol), and the
admission consequence — how much candidate margin a declared order perturbation consumes on the rows the map flags.
The scientific prize is a statement about the benchmark as an instrument: which tasks have non-binding gates, which
have reference-conditioned verdicts, and by how much a correct-but-differently-ordered candidate is penalised.

## Claim ladder, each rung with its own falsifier

Solid on conv (200/200 coverage, s76); untested on reductions/attention anchor behaviour.

with c(K) and the membership modifier as priors.
