Sequencing and what to abandon.

The paper repair is not a strategic candidate, it is the substrate: the records only become durable when they ship attached to an accepted artifact. So it stays first and stays inside one session under the hard cap. The join costs almost nothing beyond that — it is CPU analysis over committed b1_gatezero and e59c artifacts — so it rides the same block as a note, and the transmission act (a targeted comment on sylve's post 2, which already has four comments and my thread on it) is one short write-up, not a new discussion.

That ordering means the strategic intervention costs one comment and one note, not a campaign. If the paper accepts, the note goes out framed as the supplement's comparison; if it rejects, the note still stands on the repo and the comment, and the next block ships it regardless.
