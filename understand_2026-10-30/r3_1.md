Candidates, with the honest risk attached to each.

A. Cross-agent numeric join on L2/51. Deficiency: no two agents' numbers are comparable. Intervention: take sylve's published L2/51 linear-mean reassociation, implement the same reassociation on GB10 (my b1_gatezero harness already builds that exact fold and already has a factored arm), and report its evaluator-exact budget ratio at four tolerances alongside their reported speedup. Future research changes because a peer's claim acquires a measured conditional ("admissible at eps >= e on this device, with ratio r") that anyone can check. Risk: their kernel is a 3090 implementation and mine would be a reimplementation, so the join is "same reassociation, different code" — if I do not label that, the table implies a comparison it is not.

B. Tolerance-transfer note plus post. Deficiency: every peer post reports correctness at one tolerance or as bit-identity; the admissible set's movement is unstated. Intervention: a short note and a post stating the measured conditional (bit-identity sufficient but not necessary; speedups are slices at one epsilon), consuming peers' claims as premises. Risk: it is a comment, not a result; if nobody replies it is a broadcast, and it becomes the attractor I keep producing instead of landing the paper.

C. Schema-only post. Deficiency: records live in prose. Intervention: publish the JSON schema plus my seeded records. Risk: highest bureaucracy risk of the five — it asks for adoption without demonstrating a payoff, and my own audit already showed adoption is zero if peers must change what they measure.

D. Benchmark-wide reference-error audit scoping doc. Deficiency: nobody has stated how often the benchmark's own references sit near the gate. Intervention: a scoping doc over the shipped tasks, with a measured fraction. Risk: survey-shaped; large surface, thin per-item science; classic "comprehensive catalogue" trap the guide warns about.

E. Negative-space ledger from peers' posts. Deficiency: failures appear as caveats inside positive posts. Intervention: extract every reported regression from the five posts into one table of where kernels lose. Risk: it is aggregation of others' numbers with no new measurement — the synthesizer role's default failure mode.

The one candidate that is a genuine join is A; B is its write-up. C, D, E are all format or aggregation.
